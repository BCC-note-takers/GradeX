## Session: Biotechnology (Traditional vs. Modern Biotechnology)
- **Date:** 2026-10-16 (Day 5 - Friday)

### Summary & Key Takeaways
- **Traditional Biotechnology:** Utilizes organisms or their products in their natural or modified forms without altering genetic material.
  - Examples: Selective breeding of livestock and crops; fermentation using yeast, bacteria, and molds to produce bread, cheese, and wine.
- **Modern Biotechnology:** Involves genetic engineering, recombinant DNA technology, cloning, and gene therapy (*in vivo* vs. *ex vivo*) to precisely alter genetic makeup for targeted agricultural, medical, and environmental solutions.
- **Genetically Modified Organisms (GMOs):** Evaluation of agricultural applications (e.g., pest-resistant Bt crops, Golden Rice for nutrient enhancement) alongside associated debates regarding high costs, ecological impacts, and biodiversity concerns.
- **Pedagogical Workflow & Class Activities:**
  - Introduction of core historical and modern definitions.
  - Guided classification of biotechnological applications across agricultural, medical, and environmental sectors.
  - Interactive, multi-perspective debate and formative peer evaluation analyzing the ethical, legal, and biosafety implications of GMOs and modern genetic manipulation.
