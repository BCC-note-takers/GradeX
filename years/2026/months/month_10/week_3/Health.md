## Session: Overview of Community Health Programs
- **Date:** 2026-10-16 (Day 5 - Friday)

### Summary & Key Takeaways
- Community health programs address various public health concerns through distinct initiatives, guided by legal standards and protective frameworks.
- Republic Act No. 10354 (Responsible Parenthood and Reproductive Health Act):
  - Protects reproductive health and rights.
  - Mandates accurate information and services regarding reproductive health, including maternal care and family planning.
  - Ensures parents are informed about responsible parenting and family size.
  - Mandates age-appropriate reproductive health education starting from high school.
  - Prioritizes prenatal care, postnatal care, and skilled attendance during childbirth to prevent maternal and infant complications.
  - Promotes access to family planning information and services for pregnancy spacing.
- Republic Act No. 9211 (Tobacco Regulation Act):
  - Regulates the sale, advertisement, and promotion of tobacco products to protect public health, especially youth.
  - Requires health warnings on tobacco packages.
  - Restricts smoking in public places to shield non-smokers from secondhand smoke.
  - Prohibits the sale of tobacco products to minors (under 18 years old).
