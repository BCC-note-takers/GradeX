- **Date:** 2026-10-16 (Day 5 - Friday)

### Summary & Key Takeaways
- **Opening Activity & Test Instructions:** Instructions provided for a 30-item summative test focusing on points of view and contextual analysis.
- **Review of Narrative Points of View:**
  - **First-Person:** Narrator is a character in the story using pronouns like *I*, *me*, and *we*.
  - **Second-Person:** Narrator speaks directly to the reader using pronouns like *you*, *your*, and *yours*.
  - **Third-Person Limited:** Narrator is outside the story and follows the thoughts of only one character.
  - **Third-Person Omniscient:** Narrator follows the thoughts and feelings of multiple or all characters.
- **Contextual Analysis in Literature:**
  - **Biographical Context:** The author's background, experiences, and history reflected in the story.
  - **Socio-Cultural Context:** Social norms, cultural beliefs, values, and societal structures (gender, race, class, family roles) influencing the characters and storyline.
  - **Historical Context:** The specific time period, historical events, political movements, or major social changes shaping the work's creation and meaning.
