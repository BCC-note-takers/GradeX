### Summary & Key Takeaways
Summary & Key Takeaways
 * RA 8293 (Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines): Protects the rights of creators, authors, and artists over their original creations; creators must memorize and know their rights under RA 8293.
 * Copyright Ownership: Grants creators exclusive control over their original works (such as songs, scripts, and artwork) so others cannot use or reproduce them without permission.
 * Common Copyright Issues:
   * Music Piracy: Unauthorized copying, downloading, or distribution of music.
   * Sampling without Permission: Using snippets, melodies, or lyrics from another artist's song in a new recording without proper authorization.
   * Choreography Copying: Using or claiming another choreographer's original dance routines as one's own without permission.
   * Script Plagiarism / Re-use: Using or presenting someone else's written script as one's own without permission.
 * Copyright vs. Copyleft:
   * Copyright: Protects exclusive ownership, requiring permission and licensing to copy or modify.
   * Copyleft: Allows creators to give public permission for others to use, modify, and share their work, typically under specific conditions (e.g., proper credit).
 * Freedom of Expression & Censorship:
   * Freedom of expression is protected under Article III, Section 4 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution.
   * Expressing ideas is not absolute; it is subject to legal restrictions and regulatory review (e.g., MTRCB ratings and censorship to prevent harm).
   * Types of censorship: Government Censorship (e.g., MTRCB ratings/bans), Institutional Censorship (e.g., networks/schools restricting content), Self-Censorship (creators withholding ideas out of fear), and Community/Social Censorship (public pressure to alter content).
 * Other Relevant Philippine Laws:
   * RA 9165 (Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act): Relevant to media content depicting or regulating dangerous drugs.
   * RA 7394 (Consumer Act of the Philippines): Protects consumers from unsafe products and misleading content/advertising.
   * Environmental Laws: Laws promoting environmental awareness, education, and protection.
