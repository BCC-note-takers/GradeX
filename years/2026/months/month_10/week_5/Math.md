### Summary & Key Takeaways
- Discussion on measures of position in statistics, focusing on quartiles, deciles, and percentiles.
- Quartiles divide a distribution into four equal parts, identifying the first quartile ($Q_1$), second quartile ($Q_2$), and third quartile ($Q_3$).
- Deciles divide a distribution into ten equal parts ($D_1$ to $D_9$).
- Percentiles divide a distribution into one hundred equal parts ($P_1$ to $P_{99}$).
- Formulas presented for finding position: $Q_k = \frac{kN}{4}$ for quartiles, $D_k = \frac{kN}{10}$ for deciles, and $P_k = \frac{kN}{100}$ for percentiles.
- Step-by-step procedure demonstrated: arranging data sets in ascending order (from lowest to highest), determining $N$, and applying the formula to find the specific measure.
