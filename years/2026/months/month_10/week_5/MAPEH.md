Philippine Creative Industries
Philippine Creative Industries are industries that use Filipino creativity, talent, culture, and ideas to create products, services, and experiences.
PEFORMING ARTS
	Music – singers, musicians, composers
	Dance – dancers and choreographers
	Theater – actors and theater practitioners
	Musical theater – acting + singing + dance
	Opera – singing + acting + orchestral music

	Spoken word – poetry and expressive performance
	Puppetry – storytelling through puppets

How Technology Is Used in Creative Industries
1.	Recording - Technology captures sound, movement, or performance.
2.	Digital Music Production - Technology is used to create, arrange, edit, and mix music.
3.	Video Recording and Editing - Cameras and editing software are used to produce videos.
4.	Online Streaming - Technology allows audiences to watch or listen to creative works online.
5.	Social Media - Artists use platforms to share their work and interact with audiences.
6.	Digital Promotion - Technology helps artists advertise performances, songs, films, or other creative products.

	Music Piracy - Using or distributing music without proper authorization.
	Unauthorized Use - Using a song, choreography, or script without permission.

3. FREEDOM OF EXPRESSION
	The right to express ideas, feelings, and opinions through speech, writing, music, dance, and theater.
	Protected under Article III, Section 4 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution.
	The government cannot unjustly restrict freedom of speech and expression.
	This freedom is subject to lawful limitations and does not mean that everything is permitted.

4. CENSORSHIP
Censorship is the restriction, removal, or changing of artistic content by the government, institutions, communities, or the artists themselves.

Types of Censorship
Type	Simple explanation	Example
Government censorship	Restrictions imposed by government laws or agencies	The MTRCB classifies a film or television program based on its content.
Institutional censorship	Restrictions imposed by networks, producers, or platforms	A TV network asks a singer to change certain lyrics before performing.
Self-censorship	Artists limit or change their own work because they fear negative consequences	A songwriter avoids a controversial political topic because they fear losing sponsors.
Community or social censorship	Pressure from audiences or groups to remove or change content	Public criticism leads a theater group to reconsider a controversial scene.

The Role of the MTRCB
The Movie and Television Review and Classification Board (MTRCB) reviews and classifies films and television programs in the Philippines. Its classifications help audiences, especially parents, determine whether content is appropriate for particular age groups.
5. CULTURAL APPROPRIATION
Cultural appropriation is the use of elements from another culture—such as its dances, music, costumes, instruments, rituals, or symbols—without sufficient understanding, respect, acknowledgment, or permission when it is appropriate to seek it.
Effects of Cultural Appropriation on Artists and Communities
	Loss or distortion of cultural meaning: A sacred ritual may be presented as ordinary entertainment without explaining its significance.
	Lack of recognition or benefits: Artists or companies may profit from cultural elements without appropriately acknowledging or involving the originating community.
	Reinforcement of stereotypes: Indigenous peoples may be portrayed inaccurately or as exotic, primitive, or belonging only to the past.

COMMUNITY HEALTH PROGRAMS, LAWS, AND POLICIES
Community health refers to the health and well-being of people living in a particular community. It focuses on preventing diseases, promoting healthy behaviors, protecting the environment, and providing health services and programs that address the needs of the community.
Why Is Community Health Important?
1.	Prevents diseases
2.	Promotes healthy lifestyles
3.	Protects the environment
4.	Provides health services and information
5.	Addresses community health concerns
6.	Improves quality of life

Common Community Health Issues and Concerns
1. Healthy aging - This refers to creating environments and opportunities that enable people to be and  do what they value throughout their lives. It is not being free of disease or infirmity. Many older adults have certain health conditions that, when well-controlled, have little influence on their well-being.
2. Caring for the elderly - It is a community health concern to help the elderly maintain an independent life in the community for as long as possible. Community members, together with the healthcare team, play an important role and work together to ensure that appropriate services are provided to meet the varied needs of the elderly in the community.
3. Coping with loss or grief - Losing someone important to you can be difficult. You may experience grief and a wide range of emotions that change over time. There is no right or wrong way to feel. For older adults who are socially isolated or have limited family nearby, there are resources available, including the following:
	Online support groups and counseling sessions - There are online forums or support groups that may be accessed for free and can help provide support from home.
	Community resources - some communities offer grief and Community resources loss counseling or resources. These may be provided by local community centers, churches, senior citizen centers, or hospitals.
4. Building active lifestyles - Communities designed to be activity-friendly can thrive by supporting economic development, revitalization, social connection, aging in place, and long-term investments. People with limited access to places that support being active, such as sidewalks, trails, and parks, are also less likely to meet recommended physical activity levels.
5. Nurturing healthy and sustainable environments - Community health improves when people care for the elderly, live healthy lifestyles, cope well with grief, age healthily, and protect the environment, as these are all interconnected and support one another.
