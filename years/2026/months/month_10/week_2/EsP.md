## Session: The Nicene Creed, Christian Stewardship, and 'Ang Kuwento ni Budol'
- **Date:** 2026-10-06 (Day 2 - Tuesday)

### Summary & Key Takeaways
- **The Nicene Creed and Creation:**
  - Recalls the Nicene Creed (*"maker of heaven and earth, of all things visible and invisible"*) highlighting that Christian faith encompasses both seen and unseen dimensions.
  - **God the Father as Initiator:** Genesis 1:3 (*"Let there be light"*) illustrates God's ultimate authority and role as the initiator of creation.
- **Trinitarian Roles in Creation & Salvation:**
  - **God the Father:** Initiator (Genesis 1:3).
  - **God the Son (Jesus):** Agent of creation and redeemer (John 1:3, Colossians 1:16–17); sacrificial role is vital for salvation.
  - **God the Holy Spirit:** Sustainer and active force giving life daily (Genesis 1:2).
- **Call to Christian Stewardship & Three Pillars of Responsibility:**
  - **Stewardship:** Being responsible caretakers of God's creation and each other (e.g., Feeding of the 5,000, Genesis 1:28, St. Teresa's insight on simple acts with great love).
  - **Pillar 1 (For Oneself):** Accountable to God for one's life and return.
  - **Pillar 2 (For Others):** Based on Genesis 4:9 (*"Am I my brother's keeper?"*), emphasizing unselfishness and capacity to give.
  - **Pillar 3 (For the Environment):** Caring for our "common home" (*Laudato Si'*), noting how environmental harm disproportionately affects the poor.
- **Eucharistic Connection:** Communion with Christ (John 17:11) obliges believers to be sensitive to others' physical and spiritual needs.
- **Author Background ("Ang Kuwento ni Budol"):**
  - **Author:** John Brix (from Frisco, Quezon; AB in Literary and Cultural Studies at PUP).
  - **Style:** Modern poem (*makabagong tula*).
  - **Awards & Recognition:** 2024 National Youth Achievement Award (NCCA), 2024 Palanca Awards, 2021 Maningning Miclat Prize, 2022 Premio Villa, 2023 Gawad Bienvenido Lumbera, and international presentations at Birkbeck, University of London.
