## Session: Creative Industries, Performing Arts, and Technology
- **Date:** 2026-10-06 (Day 2 - Tuesday)

### Summary & Key Takeaways
- **Case Studies & Ethical Issues in Creative Work:**
  - **Unlawful Commercial Use:** Copyright violation where a brand uses an artist's (Maya's) song in an advertisement without permission or payment.
  - **Plagiarism / Content Theft:** An indie artist posting and claiming Maya's song as their own on another website.
  - **Censorship / Creative Compromise:** Choosing between a larger network television platform with mandatory lyric changes (censorship) or refusing to protect artistic integrity.
- **Definition of Creative Industries:**
  - Sectors of economic activity producing goods and services generated through human creativity, talent, skills, and intellectual resources.
  - **Examples:** Culinary, design, performing arts, media, music, film and television, animation, advertising, publishing.
- **Fields of Performing Arts:**
  - **Music:** Songs and performances by singers, musicians, or composers.
  - **Dance:** Movements by one or more dancers.
  - **Theater:** Dramatic presentations on stage by actors.
  - **Musical Theater:** Combining speaking, singing, and dancing.
  - **Opera:** Theatrical production combining music, drama, and orchestra.
  - **Circus:** Acrobatics, clowning, juggling, and magic.
  - **Spoken Word:** Recitation of poetry or spoken performances.
  - **Puppetry:** Using puppets to tell stories or act out plays.
- **Influence of Technology on Creative Industries:**
  - Expanded reach from physical stages to global online streaming services.
  - **Key Processes:** Recording, digital music production, video recording and editing, online streaming, social media engagement, and digital promotion.
