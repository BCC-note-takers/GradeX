## Session: Community Health Programs & Well-being
- **Date:** 2026-10-10 (Day 6 - Saturday)

### Summary & Key Takeaways
- **Healthy Aging:**
  - Addresses the changing healthcare and daily requirements of elderly community members.
  - Emphasizes accessible community health center services, regular check-ups, and safe public spaces for physical and social participation.
- **Caring for the Elderly:**
  - Ensures proper support, medical management (e.g., blood pressure monitoring), and nutrition for older adults to maintain independence and safety at home.
  - Highlights shared community responsibility involving families, barangay health workers, local health centers, and neighbors.
- **Coping with Loss or Grief:**
  - Supports individuals suffering from bereavement, which affects emotional, mental, and physical well-being.
  - **Resources:** Online Support Groups (remote sharing and guidance) and Community & Faith-Based Resources (local health centers, hospitals, churches, faith communities, and senior organizations).
- **Building an Active Lifestyle:**
  - Encourages physical activity through supportive community infrastructure (safe walkways, parks, playgrounds, and sports venues like basketball courts and Zumba areas).
  - Student Andrew noted that without supportive environments, individuals cannot easily exercise or maintain good health.
- **Community Health Programs & Environmental Care:**
  - **Purok Kalusugan:** Health workers bring services (vaccinations, screenings, maternal care, health education) directly to local neighborhoods (*puroks*).
  - **Botika ng Barangay (BnB):** Provides affordable essential medicines within the community.
  - **3 Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle):** Waste management initiatives protecting environmental and community health.
  - **Community Dance / Zumba Sessions:** Group exercise promoting physical and mental well-being across all ages.
- **Common Challenges in Implementation:**
  - **Limited Medical Supplies:** Shortages of medicines, vaccines, and basic equipment.
  - **Inadequate Facilities:** Lack of proper, clean examination and care spaces.
  - **Shortage of Healthcare Workers:** Scarcity of trained nurses, staff, and community health workers.

## Session: Community Health Laws & Concerns
- **Date:** 2026-10-08 (Day 4 - Thursday)

### Summary & Key Takeaways
- **Laws, Policies, & Disease Prevention:**
  - **Community Health Laws:** Established to promote, protect, and safeguard community safety and well-being.
  - **Disease Prevention:** Vaccination and immunization drives prevent infectious disease spread and control outbreaks.
  - **Dangerous Drugs & Substance Abuse:** Strict national laws penalize illegal substances (marijuana and *shabu*) with fines ranging from ₱200,000 to ₱500,000 and long-term imprisonment. While medical marijuana is used abroad, non-medical use remains strictly illegal in the Philippines.
- **Definition & Core Objectives of Community Health:**
  - **Core Goal:** Collective efforts to protect, maintain, and improve public health, safety, and quality of life.
  - **Environmental Health:** Ensures clean water, safe air, and proper waste management (using the 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle).
  - **Health Services:** Delivers accessible health education, consultations, and essential care.
- **Key Community Health Concerns & Initiatives:**
  - **Healthy Aging & Elderly Care:** Physical activity, routine check-ups (blood pressure monitoring), and community/family support.
  - **Coping with Loss & Grief:** Utilizing online support groups, counseling, and church/community resources for bereavement.
  - **Active Lifestyle Promotion:** Organized activities (fun runs, sports leagues, dance classes) and accessible parks.
- **Key Vocabulary Glossary:** Community Health, Immunization/Vaccination, Dangerous Drugs Act, 3Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle), Healthy Aging, Bereavement Support.
