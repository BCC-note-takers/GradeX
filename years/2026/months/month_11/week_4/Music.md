2. Performance Format

💻 Digital Technologies & Equipment in MAPEH Performances
3. Digital Cameras
 * Function: Used to capture high-resolution still photographs and video recordings of student performances.
 * Application: Essential for documenting live presentations, such as group dance numbers, musical plays, or singing performances in class.
4. Editing Applications
 * Function: Software or applications used to cut, combine, and enhance video and audio files.
 * Features: Allows creators to merge multiple clips, apply transitions, synchronize background music, and insert titles or lyrics.
 * Examples: CapCut, TikTok, and Canva video editing tools.
5. Sound Systems
 * Function: Used to produce music, amplify voices, and manage sound effects so that the audience can clearly hear the performance.
 * Examples: Microphones, audio mixers, and speakers used during stage presentations or competitions.
6. Lighting Equipment
 * Function: Highlights performers and creates different moods, highlights, or focal points on stage.
 * Difference from Projectors/Screens: While projectors display backgrounds, lighting equipment physically illuminates the performers and the space.
 * Examples: Spotlights used to focus audience attention on the lead dancer or lead singer.
7. Animation and Visual Effects
 * Function: Adds moving images, special effects, and digital designs to make performances more creative and engaging.
 * Examples: Adding animated stars, falling snow, or dynamic background fireworks/visuals to complement a performance.
🎨 Aesthetic Principles & Definitions
 * Aesthetic Principles (Guidelines): Principles used in art to organize elements in an artwork or performance, ensuring the final output is beautiful, meaningful, organized, and enjoyable to watch.
 * Etymology of "Aesthetic": Comes from the Greek word aisthesis, which means perception, sensory perception, or sensation. In simple terms, it refers to how we perceive, see, hear, or experience things through our senses.
